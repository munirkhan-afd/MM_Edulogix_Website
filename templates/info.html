Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MM Edulogix handles information when schools use its website and school management software.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, information may include school name and contact details, administrator and teacher account details, student records, attendance records, student photographs, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide school management features, maintain accounts, support attendance and student records, communicate important service notices, provide customer support, and protect the platform from misuse.

3. Student Information and Photographs

Schools are responsible for ensuring they have the appropriate authority and permissions to submit student information and photographs. Schools should only add information needed for legitimate school administration.

4. Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Access should be limited to authorised users and account credentials must be kept confidential.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not make school records public. Information may be processed by hosting or technical service providers only as needed to operate the service, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Account Closure

Records may be retained while a school account is active and for a reasonable period needed for service, security, legal, or backup purposes. Subscription expiry does not by itself mean that school records are deleted.

7. Access and Corrections

Schools should contact the Admin/Owner to request help with access, correction, or account-related privacy questions. A school remains responsible for the accuracy and lawful handling of information it enters.

8. Policy Updates and Contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. For privacy questions, contact the MM Edulogix Admin/Owner through WhatsApp.
